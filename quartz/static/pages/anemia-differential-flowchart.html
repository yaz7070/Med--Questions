Anemia Differential Flowchart

Work top to bottom. Confirm anemia, sort by MCV, then follow your column. Every hemolysis branch ends in the hemolysis module below.

Question Order this Diagnosis Answer branch label
STEP 1

Confirm anemia

Hb below lab reference range: < 135 g/L men, < 115 g/L women.
Check WBC and platelets now. Pancytopenia points to marrow (aplastic, MDS, myelophthisis, B12/folate).
STEP 2

Sort by MCV

< 80 fL micro · 80 to 100 fL normo · > 100 fL macro
Lam's macrocytic slides use > 97 fL. Hemolysis presents at any MCV.
STEP 3

Read the film and retics

Order Reticulocyte count and Peripheral blood film on every case.
High retics = marrow responding (bleed or hemolysis). Low/normal = production problem.

Microcytic

MCV < 80 · TAILS · low heme or low globin
  • Serum ferritin (first test)
    • < 30Iron deficiency anemia
      Ferritin < 30: sens 92%, spec 98%. Low Fe, high TIBC, low TSAT, high sTfR, high RDW, high platelets, pencil cells.
      • Find the cause
        Intake (vegan) · absorption (celiac, gastritis, H. pylori, bypass) · demand (pregnancy) · loss (menses, fibroids, GI bleed, PNH/valve, donation, marathon)
    • 30 to 100Inflammation present?
      • sTfR / log ferritin
        • > 2ACI + true iron deficiency
        • < 1Anemia of chronic inflammation
    • > 100 + inflammationAnemia of chronic inflammation
      Hepcidin up, ferroportin blocked. Low Fe, low/normal transferrin. MCV rarely < 75. Infection, RA/SLE/IBD, cancer.
    • Normal, no inflammationWhich clue fits?
      • RBC high, RDW normal, FHx/ethnicityThalassemia
        • Hb electrophoresis (HPLC)
          • HbA2 highβ-thal (Mediterranean)
          • Normalα-thal not excluded (SE Asia, Africa)
            α gene sequencing HbH inclusions
      • Lead, alcohol, B6, anti-TB, prior chemoSideroblastic / lead
        Basophilic stippling, Pappenheimer bodies. BM: ring sideroblasts

Normocytic

MCV 80 to 100 · ABCD · acute loss, BM failure, chronic disease, destruction
  • Reticulocyte count
    • HighIncreased turnover. Hemolysis labs?
      LDH · indirect bili · haptoglobin
      • LDH ↑ bili ↑ hapto ↓Hemolysis
        Go to the hemolysis module ↓
      • NormalAcute blood loss
        GI source, menses, fibroids, urinary, bleeding disorder (vWD, HHT)
    • Low / normalDecreased production. Other lines down? Systemic disease?
      • Chronic inflammation or CKDACI / renal (no BM pathology)
        Ferritin high-normal. Relative EPO deficiency.
      • Cytopenias or abnormal filmBM aspirate + biopsy
        • EmptyAplastic anemia
          Pancytopenia: bleeding, infections. Fatty marrow.
        • Empty, red onlyPure red cell aplasia
          Severe reticulocytopenia, absent erythroid precursors, no splenomegaly.
        • AbnormalMyelodysplastic syndrome
          Age 65+, prior chemo/radiation. Cytogenetics
        • ReplacedMyelophthisis
          Leukoerythroblastic film, teardrops. Myelofibrosis, leukemia/lymphoma, metastases.

Macrocytic

MCV > 100 · ABCDEF · alcohol, B12, compensatory retics, drugs/dysplasia, endocrine, folate
  • Blood film + retic count
    • Oval macrocytes, hypersegmented PMNsMegaloblastic (DNA synthesis failure)
      Serum B12 · RBC folate · MMA · homocysteine
      • MMA ↑ + Hcy ↑B12 deficiency
        Neuro signs (proprioception, neuropathy). Pernicious anemia, gastrectomy, ileal resection, Crohn's, vegan, fish tapeworm. IF antibodies
      • Hcy ↑ onlyFolate deficiency
        No neuro. Alcohol, poor diet, pregnancy, hemolysis, anticonvulsants, jejunal resection.
      • Both normalDrug or marrow?
        • Drug HxDrug-induced
          Methotrexate, trimethoprim, hydroxyurea, azathioprine, nitrous oxide
        • Older, cytopeniasMDS
          Pseudo-Pelger-Huët cells. BM + cytogenetics
    • Round macrocytes, normal PMNsNon-megaloblastic
      • Retics highReticulocytosis
        Bleed or hemolysis. Go to hemolysis module ↓
      • Target cellsLiver disease
        LFTs Excess membrane lipid, hypersplenism.
      • Alcohol HxAlcohol
        MCV 100 to 110, low platelets. Check for mixed deficiencies.
      • Cold, constipated, slow HRHypothyroidism
        TSH Linked with pernicious anemia.
      • Rouleaux or clumpsPseudo-macrocytosis
        Myeloma (rouleaux), cold agglutinin disease (agglutination).
Module

Hemolysis confirmed

Labs: LDH ↑, indirect bilirubin ↑, haptoglobin ↓, retics ↑. Intravascular adds hemoglobinemia (pink plasma) and hemoglobinuria (brown urine).

  • DAT (direct Coombs)
    • Positive · IgGWarm AIHA (70%)
      Spherocytes, extravascular. Idiopathic 60%. Look for CLL/lymphoma, SLE, drugs (penicillin, quinidine, methyldopa). Steroids, then splenectomy.
    • Positive · C3Cold agglutinin disease (30%)
      IgM, agglutination. Mycoplasma, mono, LPD, hepatitis. Treat cause, avoid cold. Steroids do not work.
    • Positive · historyAlloimmune
      Transfusion reaction (immediate ABO, delayed 5 to 10 days), hemolytic disease of the newborn.
    • NegativeRead the blood film →

History cues to ask every case

  • Bleeding: menses, GI (melena), GU, ENT, recent delivery
  • Diet and GI surgery: vegan, gastrectomy, ileal or jejunal resection, celiac
  • Drugs: antimalarials, sulfonamides, methotrexate, anticonvulsants, chemo
  • Transfusions, heart valves, alcohol, old house or lead exposure
  • Family history, ethnicity, prior normal CBC
  • Chronic disease: infection, autoimmune, cancer, CKD, liver, thyroid
